idx_pickup_time exists but is NOT used -- MySQL's optimizer chose a
table scan instead. January covers ~31% of the table (3.47M of
11.2M rows); at that selectivity, a secondary (non-covering) index
means one random-access lookup per matching row to fetch the other
columns, which the optimizer correctly judged more expensive than a
sequential scan. This is a real, common outcome, not a bug -- and
it's exactly what a covering index (next stage) fixes.
